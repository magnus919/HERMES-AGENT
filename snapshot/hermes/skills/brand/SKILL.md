## Brand Identity Components

### 1. Brand Voice
- Tone of voice (professional, friendly, playful, etc.)
- Key messaging pillars
- Vocabulary and language rules

### 2. Visual Identity
- Logo (primary, secondary, icon version)
- Color palette (primary, secondary, accent)
- Typography (heading, body, accent fonts)
- Imagery style (photos, illustrations, icons)

### 3. Brand Applications
- Business cards
- Letterhead
- Website
- Social media
- Packaging
- Print materials
